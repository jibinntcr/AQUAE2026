9. AQUAEChain

Digital Infrastructure for ALCI Credits

AQUAEChain is being developed to support the digital infrastructure behind ALCI Credits through a technology services agreement with MindWave Innovations.

Under the agreement, MindWave is responsible for designing, developing, deploying and hosting the platform. AQUAE leads the ALCI methodology, business development, brand, partnerships and ecosystem engagement.

The planned platform is intended to bring together blockchain infrastructure, an ALCI registry, MRV data management, marketplace functions and insurance-related features.

Evidence-First Architecture

Connecting Digital Records with Ecological Evidence

AQUAEChain is being developed to improve the traceability of environmental credit records by linking digital records with field assessment data and other relevant information sources.

Blockchain technology can provide a record of transactions and changes in recorded ownership. However, it does not independently verify the accuracy of ecological data, the validity of valuations or a project’s environmental performance. These depend on reliable evidence, appropriate methodologies and established verification processes.

AQUAEChain’s development, deployment and operational capabilities will depend on implementation milestones, technical readiness and applicable requirements.

Why Traceability Requires Field MRV

Rather than treating environmental credits as detached digital tokens, AQUAEChain is designed to anchor every credit record to documented site profiles, MRV field datasets, valuation assumptions, and applicable insurance documentation.

Innovation and Intellectual Property

Foundational Patent Application Filed

AQUAE Labs has filed its foundational patent application, marking an important milestone in the development of its ecological intelligence and ecosystem-finance technology.

The application encompasses approximately 10–15 inventive concepts that may contribute to AQUAE Labs’ longer-term intellectual property strategy. These concepts relate to the development of innovations in natural capital accounting, ecosystem valuation and environmental markets.

The application establishes a foundation for the company’s developing intellectual property portfolio. The scope of any future patent protection will depend on the examination and outcome of the relevant applications.

The filing recognises the scientific and technological contributions of inventors Dr. F. Ranil Senanayake and Dr. Vin Menon, with the support of Linju Thomas, Chief Operating Officer, and Dejan de Zoysa, Chief Technology Officer, alongside AQUAE’s partners and legal advisers.

This milestone reflects AQUAE Labs’ continued work to develop scientific methods and technology infrastructure for assessing ecological value and supporting more transparent, evidence-based environmental markets.
