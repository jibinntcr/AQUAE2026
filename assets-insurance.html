ALCI ASSETS & RISK FRAMEWORK

Connecting Assessed Ecosystem Services with Environmental Markets

Grounded in documented ecological assessment, MRV information, digital traceability and a risk-aware insurance framework.

ALCI ASSETS

A Structured Approach to Environmental Assets

ALCI Assets are being developed as environmental assets associated with assessed ecosystem services and the ecological value represented by eligible projects.

Their intended foundation is the assessment of ecosystem functions through documented methodologies and relevant Monitoring, Recording and Verification (MRV) information. This approach aims to connect environmental asset records with the underlying ecological context rather than treating credits as detached digital assets.

ALCI Assets are intended to support the development of environmental markets in which ecological information, valuation and traceability play an important role.

ALCI brings together several connected elements:

  • Ecological assessment and valuation.
  • Monitoring, reporting and verification.
  • Documentation of environmental asset information.
  • Digital recordkeeping and traceability.
  • An insurance framework for specified risks, subject to policy terms.
  • Potential access to environmental markets, subject to applicable requirements and buyer acceptance.

The objective is to support greater transparency around the ecological basis, documentation and risk considerations associated with environmental assets.

Risk-Aware Protection

Insured ALCI Assets

Insurance is an important component of the developing ALCI offering. The insurance framework is intended to address specified risks associated with ecosystem performance and the underlying value represented by eligible ALCI assets.

Potential areas of coverage include qualifying losses in ecosystem performance, damage from specified natural hazards, biomass reversal and certain credit-delivery risks.

The actual scope of protection will depend on the applicable policy wording, underwriting approval, eligibility requirements, exclusions and agreed terms. Not all risks, financial losses or credit values are necessarily insured.

Important Valuation & Liquidity Notice: ALCI assets and their associated valuation should not be interpreted as guaranteeing investment returns, market liquidity, financial performance or complete protection against ecological loss.
INSURANCE AND RISK FRAMEWORK

A Risk-Aware Approach to Ecosystem Value

Environmental projects can be exposed to a range of risks, including natural hazards, changes in ecosystem performance and challenges affecting the delivery of expected environmental outcomes. AQUAE Impact is developing an insurance framework as part of its approach to environmental credit protection.

The framework is intended to connect the assessed value of ecosystems with structured consideration of specified risks associated with eligible ALCI Credits.

Underwriting Scope

Potential Areas of Coverage

Depending on the relevant policy and underwriting assessment, potential areas of coverage may include:

  • Qualifying losses in ecosystem performance.
  • Damage resulting from specified natural hazards.
  • Biomass reversal.
  • Certain environmental credit-delivery risks.

These are potential areas of coverage, not a representation that every risk is covered under every policy.

Integrated Architecture

How Insurance Fits into ALCI

The intended approach combines ecosystem assessment, MRV documentation, traceable digital records and insurance arrangements. Together, these elements aim to support a clearer understanding of the ecological basis of an environmental asset and the specific risks for which protection may be available.

Insurance can provide protection only within the scope of the relevant contract. Coverage is subject to policy wording, underwriting approval, eligibility requirements, exclusions, limits and other agreed conditions. Not all ecological risks, financial losses or credit values are necessarily insured, and insurance does not eliminate environmental risk.

Details of applicable coverage should be confirmed against the relevant policy documentation before any credit or project is represented as insured.
